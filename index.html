About Sai Green Nursery

Established & Experienced: Sai Green Nursery has been a pioneer in the horticulture and landscaping industry in Noida and Delhi NCR for over a decade. We started with a mission to make our urban surroundings greener and healthier.

We are one of the largest direct suppliers of Natural Grass Carpets (Mexican, Korean, Selection No. 1) grown in our own farms. By eliminating middlemen, we ensure that our customers get farm-fresh grass at wholesale prices.

Our team consists of expert horticulturists, landscape designers, and skilled gardeners (Malis) who understand soil health, plant nutrition, and aesthetic design. Whether it is a small balcony garden or a large cricket ground, we treat every project with the same dedication and care.

We don't just sell plants; we provide complete green solutions including soil preparation, manure application, pesticide treatment, and long-term maintenance.

Our Services

Complete Landscaping Solutions under one roof.

Rent a Mali (Gardener Services)

Keeping a garden healthy requires consistent care. We offer professional "Rent a Mali" services for homes and offices.

Our experienced gardeners will visit your premises to perform essential tasks: pruning, mowing, de-weeding, applying fertilizers, and checking for pests. You can hire a Mali on a daily visit basis or a monthly contract basis depending on your garden's needs.

Lawn Development

We specialize in developing lush green lawns from scratch. Our process involves ground leveling, soil enrichment (adding Okhla khad/manure), pest control treatment (Termite guard), and laying fresh grass rolls.

Within days, your barren land is transformed into a beautiful green carpet ready for use.

Vertical & Terrace Gardening

Short on space? Go vertical! We install stunning Bio-Walls (Green Walls) and vertical garden structures that are perfect for balconies and office reception areas.

We also design Terrace Gardens using lightweight pots and drip irrigation systems to reduce water usage and structural load.
